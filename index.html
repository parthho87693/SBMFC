Who We Are

MORE THAN A
MUSIC COMMUNITY.

SBMFC was born from a simple idea — music brings people together.

It began in June 2019, when a group of school students in Sylhet, united by a genuine love for music, came together to stay close to the city's evolving music scene. What started as a small circle built around a shared passion gradually became something much larger.

Over the years, SBMFC has worked alongside numerous bands, musical clubs, organizers and artists as a promotional partner — helping audiences discover upcoming shows, concerts, releases and music-related activities across Sylhet and beyond.

From a small group of students to a recognised home for audiences looking for updates about shows, concerts and music adda, SBMFC has continued to grow through the power of community. And this journey is only beginning.

Our Journey

THE JOURNEY SO FAR.

2019

The Beginning

SBMFC begins its journey in June 2019, founded by school students brought together by their passion for music and the Sylhet music scene.

2020–2021

Building The Community

An audience-driven community begins forming around music updates, shows, discussions and music adda.

2022–2023

Becoming A Promotional Partner

SBMFC begins working with bands, musical clubs and event organizers, supporting concerts and helping connect events with audiences.

2024

A Growing Music Network

SBMFC expands its collaborations and becomes increasingly connected with artists, bands and music communities beyond Sylhet.

2025–2026

Beyond Sylhet

From local concerts to collaborations involving artists and communities from across Bangladesh and beyond, SBMFC continues to expand its musical network.

2026+

The Next Chapter

FROM NOTHING TO A
HOME FOR THE AUDIENCE.

SBMFC didn't begin with a stage, a production team or a large organization behind it.

It began with students, music and a shared curiosity about what was happening in the scene.

Years later, that same curiosity has grown into a community — a place where audiences come to find out what's happening next, where concerts are discovered, and where artists and organizers can reach the people who care.

A place built around music.
